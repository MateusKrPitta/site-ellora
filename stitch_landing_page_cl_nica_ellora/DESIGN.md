## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the high-end aesthetic of an exclusive boutique aesthetic clinic, blending medical authority with the warmth, discretion, and tailored elegance of luxury hospitality. It speaks to discerning patients seeking facial harmonization, natural rejuvenation, skin health, and individualized longevity therapies.

### Aesthetic Direction: Warm Minimalist Boutique Luxury
The visual identity departs from sterile, cold medical interfaces in favor of organic warmth, earthy tonalities, generous negative space, and editorial craftsmanship.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm travertine, champagne gold, deep espresso, and soft terracotta shades create a grounded sanctuary sensation.
- **Human-Centric & Discreet:** Visual elements emphasize gentle transitions, calm layouts, and tactile warmth over high-frequency UI noise.
- **Editorial Balance:** Classical editorial serifs for refined statements paired with geometric, accessible sans-serifs for diagnostic and scheduling clarity.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in natural mineral tones, warm clays, and luminous skin undertones, establishing a sense of understated luxury and wellness.

- **Primary (`#8C583E` - Warm Terracotta Bronze):** Used for primary conversion touchpoints, active states, key interactive indicators, and distinguished framing.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Warm Champagne Ochre):** Accents subtle decorative dividers, subtle badges, badges of prestige, and highlighted quotes.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DFD8` - Nude Alabaster Tint):** Employed for surface containers, delicate card backgrounds, subtle borders, and soft interactive hover fills.
- **Neutral (`#2C2623` - Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded deep tone for primary typographic hierarchy, crisp iconography, and dark contrast elements. Replaces harsh pure black `#000000` with an organic, softer depth.
- **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7` - Porcelain Silk):** The default application background, warm and paper-like, minimizing eye strain while conveying luminous skin purity.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of **Playfair Display** with the clear, modern utility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Headlines & Display):** High-contrast letterforms convey clinical prestige, artistic harmony, and bespoke treatment plans. Titles emphasize natural elegance and quiet confidence.
- **Humanist Sans-Serif (Body & Labels):** Delivers clean readability for treatment explanations, medical dossiers, booking calendars, and consultation details.
- **Letter Spacing:** Uppercase labels and micro-copy leverage delicate positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to reinforce a couture, boutique feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed with breathing room and composure, channeling the serenity of an upscale wellness sanctuary. 

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid grid system with a maximum centered container of `1240px`, flanked by `margin` (`3rem`) and `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid grid with `2rem` outer margin and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Vertical single-column stack with `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) and `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`).

### Rhythm & Density
Whitespace is treated as an active luxury material. Section breaks use generous margins to prevent the clinical anxiety often triggered by crowded medical layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient layers rather than artificial hard drop shadows.

- **Warm Ambient Diffusion:** Shadows are tinted with deep espresso (`#2C2623` at 4% to 8% opacity) rather than pure black, mixed with generous blurs (`18px` to `32px`) to emulate soft architectural lighting.
- **Tonal Tiers:** Floating cards and diagnostic panels rest on surface layers tinted with `#FFFFFF` or pale champagne (`#FAF6F0`), bordered with ultra-fine hairpins (`1px solid rgba(140, 88, 62, 0.12)`).
- **Subtle Translucency:** Floating consultation headers and bottom action sheets employ frosted glass surfaces with high background blur (`16px`) and `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)` fill.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, organic geometric curves (`roundedness: 2`, matching `0.5rem` baseline, `1rem` on cards, and full pills on interactive chips and CTAs). 

Curvature mimics the natural contours of facial anatomy and delicate symmetry. Rigid, aggressive square corners are avoided to preserve a calming, non-intimidating aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Button:** Solid terracotta background (`#8C583E`), crisp cream text (`#FDFBF7`), `rounded-full` or `rounded-md` with `12px 28px` padding. Subtle hover shifts to slightly darker earthen bronze with smooth `200ms` ease.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with a `1px` border in `#C5A880`, text in `#2C2623`. Hover applies a delicate warm glow (`#E8DFD8` at 40%).
- **Text Link:** `label-md` uppercase styling with subtle bronze underline offset by `4px`.

### Cards & Service Spotlights
- **Treatment Cards:** Elevated with a subtle `1px` warm border (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.3)`), cream base (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` padding, and soft rounded corners.
- **Testimonial & Doctor Profile Cards:** Features soft beige background containers (`#E8DFD8` at 35% opacity) with Playfair Display italic lead-ins and discreet gold star accents.

### Form Fields & Booking Controls
- **Input Fields:** Generous touch targets (`48px` minimum height), pale warm backgrounds (`#FAF7F2`), and a refined bottom-only or full thin outline in `#D8CEC5`. Focused states subtly transition the border to `#8C583E`.
- **Date & Slot Pickers:** Minimal calendar widgets utilizing circular indicators with `#8C583E` active fills and soft champagne weekend highlights.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) components with `6px 14px` padding, rendered in soft neutral nude backgrounds (`#E8DFD8`) with bronze lettering (`#8C583E`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded form controls utilizing primary terracotta (`#8C583E`) active fill with smooth scale transitions, ensuring an intuitive, polished patient onboarding experience.
